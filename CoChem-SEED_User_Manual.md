# 🌱 CoChem-SEED: Student User Manual

**Version 1.0 | A Beginner's Guide to Your Computational Lab**

Welcome to CoChem-SEED! This manual is written specifically for you—the student. You do not need to know how to program in Python, Linux, or anything else to succeed in this lab.

This guide will explain exactly how to use the system, how you are graded, and how your privacy is protected.

## 📖 Chapter 1: Understanding Your Workspace

When you open your lab, you will be looking at a **Jupyter Notebook**. Think of this as a digital lab notebook where you can read instructions, look at 3D molecules, and press "Play" to run chemistry experiments.

### The "Play" Button (Running Cells)

The notebook is divided into blocks called **Cells**.

- Some cells just have text for you to read.
    
- Some cells have computer code that does the chemistry math.
    
- **Your Job:** You just click on a cell and press the **Play (▶) button** at the top of the screen, or press `Shift + Enter`. The computer does the rest!
    

## 🧠 Chapter 2: Socratic Traps and Learning

As you run the lab, the system will pause and ask you questions about the chemistry you are seeing. We call these **Socratic Traps**.

- **What are they?** They are interactive questions designed to make you think about the reaction (like asking you to identify a transition state).
    
- **No "Cheating" the Page:** If you get stuck and try to hit the "Refresh" button on your browser to reset the question, the system will remember! CoChem-SEED takes a snapshot of your screen. When you come back, it will instantly put you right back at the exact same question.
    
- **Why?** Because making mistakes is how we learn! The system won't punish you for trying; it wants you to think through the chemistry.
    

## 📊 Chapter 3: How You Are Graded (The RAI)

Instead of just giving you a standard percentage grade, CoChem-SEED uses something called the **Research Aptitude Index (RAI)**.

- **What is it?** It's a score that measures how well you think like a scientist.
    
- **How to get a good RAI:** Read the feedback carefully. If the system corrects a mistake, try not to make the exact same mistake on the next step. The system rewards improvement and careful thought!
    

## 🔒 Chapter 4: Your Privacy is Guaranteed (FERPA)

You might wonder: _"Is my student data safe in this cloud environment?"_ **Yes. 100%.**

CoChem-SEED uses a military-grade security step called **Cryptographic Salting**.

1. When you type in your Student ID, the system instantly scrambles it into a random string of letters and numbers.
    
2. Your real Student ID is **never** saved on the computer's hard drive.
    
3. Your professor uses a special digital key to unscramble the final grades at the end of the semester.
    

## 📤 Chapter 5: Turning In Your Lab Report

In a normal computer science class, you would have to submit a messy file full of code. CoChem-SEED does the cleanup for you!

At the very end of your lab, there will be a final "Export" cell (Stage 5.0).

1. Run that cell.
    
2. CoChem-SEED will automatically strip away all the ugly Python code.
    
3. It will generate a beautiful, clean **PDF or HTML file** that only contains your chemistry graphs, 3D molecule pictures, and the answers you typed.
    
4. You simply download that PDF and upload it to Canvas (or your school's grading system).
    

You are now ready to start your first lab! Happy discovering!
